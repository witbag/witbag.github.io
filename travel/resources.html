Travel Websites

General planning:
Wikivoyage: Free online guide
UNESCO World Heritage Sites (a more detailed map here)
Sightsmap: Photographed places (website seems dead). Similar map for sports activities here, here, here. Also here.
Dots on Maps: Maps of places to visit. Also here. Megalith map.
OpenClimateMap: Monthly climate maps (also here, here)
Windy: Weather forecast. More here, here, here, here. Past weather here
Airly: Air quality map (also here, here, here, here, here)
Copernicus Browser: Live satellite images (useful for checking snow cover). Also here. Also webcams
OpenMaps: Hiking maps. More here, OpenTopoMap, here, here, here, here, here, here
Topographic maps
Old topographic maps: Soviet (also here, here, here). Perry–Castañeda Library. More here, here, here.
Wikiloc: Hiking trails. More here, here, here
Summitpost: Hiking and climbing. More here, here.
Happycow: Map of vegetarian food locations.
Falling Fruit: Freegan map.
Dumpstermap: Dumpster diving map
iOverlander: Practical information for overland travel
Visa requirements on Wikipedia: See which countries you need a visa for (also here).
Lonely Planet maps: Maps from Lonely Planet guidebooks (without legends).
Library Genesis, Anna's Archive: For finding travel guide ebooks.
Cicerone Press: Good hiking guidebooks.
The Hiking Life: Good hiking blog (except the super expensive "gear" recommendations).
Besthike, Walkopedia: Lists of famous (not necessarily "best") hikes.
Nomadwiki: Used to have useful travel tips but most articles are now gone. View the archived version.
Travelpod: Used to be the best travel resource until it got bought and shut down by the evil Tripadvisor.
Thorn Tree forum: Another great resource, shut down in 2020.

Transport:
Rome2rio: Find bus, train, and ferry connections
Seat 61: Information about train travel
ÖPNVKarte: Maps of public transport routes
Hitchwiki: Hitchhiking guide and other good advice. See their maps of hitchhiking spots (also here, here, here).
Flightconnections: Airline route maps (also here).
Azair: The best search engine for low-cost airlines but it seems no longer maintained. Not reliable outside Europe.
Skiplagged: Flights search engine. Good for finding "hidden-city" flights (mainly in the US)
Skyscanner: Flights search engine. Changing the country and currency sometimes gives lower prices.
Kayak: Flights search engine
Google: Flights search engine
Fly4free: Blog featuring cheap flight deals
